MODULE 12

💼 Build Websites for Clients

Learn how to turn your website skills into a service.

TOPIC 01

Choose a Type of Client

Many small businesses and individuals need simple websites.

Examples include:

  • Small businesses
  • Restaurants
  • Salons
  • Event planners
  • Personal brands
  • Coaches
  • Photographers
  • Local service providers

TOPIC 02

Understand the Client's Needs

Before coding, ask what the client wants.

  • What does the business do?
  • Who are the customers?
  • What pages are needed?
  • What information should appear?
  • Are images available?
  • What contact details should be displayed?

TOPIC 03

Create a Simple Plan

Write down the pages before starting.

About
Services
Portfolio
Contact

TOPIC 04

Build a Professional Website

Use the skills from previous modules:

  • HTML structure
  • CSS design
  • Responsive layout
  • JavaScript interaction
  • Images
  • Navigation
  • Contact links

TOPIC 05

Communicate Clearly

Good communication is part of professional web design.

Tell the client what you need, what you will deliver, and when they can expect the project.

TOPIC 06

Create a Portfolio

Build sample websites even before you get your first client.

Your portfolio demonstrates what you can do.
