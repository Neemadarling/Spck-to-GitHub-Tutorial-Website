MODULE 13

💰 How to Price & Sell Your Websites

Learn practical ways to present, price and sell your website services.

TOPIC 01

Understand Your Value

You are not only charging for typing code. You are providing a solution.

Your work can save a client time, improve their online presence and help them present their business professionally.

TOPIC 02

Consider the Project Scope

Before giving a price, consider:

  • Number of pages
  • Design complexity
  • Number of sections
  • Images and content
  • Forms
  • Animations
  • Special features
  • Hosting or publishing assistance

TOPIC 03

Create Packages

You can create different service levels.

Starter

Simple website for a small business or personal brand.

Professional

More pages, stronger design and additional features.

Custom

A larger project based on the client's specific needs.

Your exact prices should depend on your skills, market, project scope and agreement with the client.

TOPIC 04

Explain Your Price

Do not simply send a number. Explain what the client receives.

For example: 

Website design
5 pages
Mobile-friendly layout
Contact section
Social media links
Basic animations
Publishing assistance

TOPIC 05

Finding Clients

You can show your portfolio through channels where potential customers can find you.

  • WhatsApp
  • Facebook
  • TikTok
  • Instagram
  • Local business networks
  • Personal referrals

TOPIC 06

Professional Communication

Be clear about:

  • What you will build
  • Price
  • Payment arrangement
  • Delivery timeline
  • Number of revisions
  • What is included

PRACTICAL PROJECT

💼 Create Your Website Service Package

Write your own service package with:

  1. Package name
  2. What is included
  3. Starting price
  4. Delivery expectations
  5. Contact method

  3. Why is a portfolio important?
  4. What makes communication professional?
  5. Name three places where you could find clients.
